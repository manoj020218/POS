Frequently asked

Questions shop owners actually ask

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. Billing, cart, discounts, and receipt printing all run entirely on the tablet itself. When your internet comes back — even hours later — everything you sold syncs up automatically. Nothing is lost.

What hardware do I need?

An Android tablet (most tablets from the last several years work fine) and, if you want printed receipts, a Bluetooth or USB thermal receipt printer. That's it — no special POS terminal or cash register required.

Is my shop's data safe and private?

Yes. Every shop's data is completely separate — another business using Smart POS can never see your sales, customers, products, or stock. Your data belongs to your shop alone.

Can more than one counter or cashier use it at the same time?

Yes. Add as many terminals as you have counters, and as many cashier logins as you need. They all share the same product list, customers, and stock, and sync together.

What does it actually cost?

Smart POS is free to use. Sign up, install the app, and start billing — no subscription, no card required.

I already have hundreds of products — do I have to enter them one by one?

No. Smart POS supports bulk import from a spreadsheet (CSV) file, including straight from a USB pen drive on your tablet, so you can load your whole catalog in one go.

What if my printer isn't connected or breaks mid-sale?

The sale still completes and saves normally — Smart POS shows the full receipt on screen so you can still tell the customer their total, and you can retry printing once the printer's back.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to set this up?

No. Sign up, install the app, sign in with the credentials you're given, and you're ready to bill. Pairing a printer is a one-time, guided step from within the app.
